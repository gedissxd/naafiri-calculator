Overall Showdown — A vs B

What goes into each row
Poke damage
Total of the Poke · Q → Q combo: both Darkin Daggers on the same target plus the Packmates that leap in (2 bites each).
Trade damage
Total of the Short trade · Q → E → AA → Q combo, with Packmates, item procs and runes.
Burst damage
Total of the Full burst · W → R → AA → Q → E → AA → Q combo. The Call of the Pack is active (+22% AD, +2 Packmates) and every item proc and rune fires once.
Sustained DPS
The damage rows of the Sustained output / second card, summed: Q + E on their effective cooldowns, basic attacks and Packmate bites.
Q heal / s
Darkin Daggers' recast heal (rank base + 45% bonus AD) divided by Q's effective cooldown.
Durability
Naafiri's effective health at the selected level: Health × (1 + (Armor + Magic Resist) ÷ 200), i.e. the average of her effective HP against physical and magic damage. Shields and spell shields are not counted.
Move speed
Out-of-combat move speed after soft caps (340 base + boots, Youmuu's Haunt, % bonuses).
Gold cost
Total gold spent on each build. The bar fills toward the more expensive build; the number is the raw gold difference between the two (not a percent).
Each bar's % = how much more the leading build does than the other ((leader − loser) ÷ loser) — except Gold cost, whose number is the flat gold gap. All damage is after the target's armor, so the result changes with the target you pick.
